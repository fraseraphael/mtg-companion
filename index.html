Deck Library

Every deck, every card — browse as lists with real mana symbols or full-art galleries, with instant card previews on hover.

Play Guides

Per-deck strategy: game plans by turn, key cards, mana curves, and what to protect. Skim one before you shuffle up.

Life Counter

Up to four players, commander damage, face-to-face screen rotation, dice, and a first-player spinner. Phone-first.
