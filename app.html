How do I get my decklist as text?
From Moxfield (phone or PC):
  1. Open your deck and tap the ⋯ More button
  2. Choose Export
  3. Pick the Text (or "MTG Arena") format and hit Copy
  4. Come back here and paste it in the box below
The text just looks like a shopping list — a number and a card name per line:
Commander 1 Atraxa, Praetors' Voice Deck 1 Sol Ring 1 Cultivate 10 Forest
Archidekt, TappedOut and most other deck sites have the same Export → Text option, and their formats work here too. Extra stuff like set codes "(C21) 263" is fine — I'll clean it up. Sideboards are ignored automatically. If there's no "Commander" line, I'll ask you to pick the commander after reading the list.
