    How to play

    Play your turn, press the big button, the table does the rest: the dummy takes its turn, you untap and draw.

    • Tap or drag a card from your hand onto the table to play it. Lit cards are ones you can pay for now; mana is tapped for you.
    • Tap a land on the table to float its mana by hand.
    • Long-press / right-click any card for more: cast anyway, card text, move it.
    • Combat: tap creatures to choose attackers, then press the button.
